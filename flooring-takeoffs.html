FAQTrade-specific
Do you follow the finish schedule or the plans?
Both — quantities are measured from the plans and reconciled against the finish schedule and finish legend. Conflicts between the two are flagged on the assumptions page so you can qualify your bid instead of eating the difference.
Can you match my bid structure?
Yes. The workbook is organized to CSI MasterFormat by default, or to your own cost codes and room/area breakdown — whatever your crews and suppliers price from.
How do I start?
Send a drawings link through the quote form or to info@sharpbid.ca. A fixed price and delivery date come back within 4 business hours.
