FAQTrade-specific
Why do glazing takeoffs start higher?
Envelope scope carries the most detail risk of any division — schedules, elevations, sections and specs all have to agree, and they often don't. The takeoff reconciles them and flags every conflict, which is exactly where bids are won or blown.
Can you break out alternates?
Yes — alternate systems or glass makeups are quantified side by side so you can price options without redoing the count.
