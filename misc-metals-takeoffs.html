FAQTrade-specific
Do you check both arch and structural sets?
Always — misc metals live in the gaps between the two. Items shown on one set and missing from the other are the classic scope trap, and they're flagged on the assumptions page.
Can quantities be organized for shop estimating?
Yes — by detail number and assembly, so your shop can price material and fab hours item by item.
