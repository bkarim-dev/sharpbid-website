FAQTrade-specific
Do you take off high-rise concrete?
SharpBid focuses on low-rise and small-site concrete scopes — the work where a fast, senior takeoff changes your bid. Full high-rise structural takeoffs are quoted case by case and only when the timeline supports doing them properly.
Is rebar included?
Rebar detailing is carried as a supplier-quote allowance and stated plainly on the assumptions page — you get accurate concrete and formwork numbers without paying for detailing you'd source elsewhere anyway.
