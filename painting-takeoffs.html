FAQTrade-specific
How do you handle spec-to-drawing conflicts?
The room finish schedule, the painting spec and the drawings are reconciled — where they disagree, the conflict is flagged so you can qualify it rather than absorb it.
Do you count repaint / occupied work differently?
Yes — repaint scope is measured with access and protection conditions noted, since your production rates depend on them.
