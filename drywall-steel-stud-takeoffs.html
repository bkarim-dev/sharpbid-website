FAQTrade-specific
Do you split labour-driving items from board count?
Yes — framing, board, insulation and finishing quantities are separated so you can apply different labour rates to each, instead of pricing everything off one blended SF number.
What about mid-rise and high-rise repetition?
Typical floors are measured once and extended with a clear multiplier logic shown in the workbook — you see exactly which levels were counted and which were extended.
